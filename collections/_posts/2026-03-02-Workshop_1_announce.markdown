The UK Data Linkage Community is delighted to announce our upcoming **Workshop for Data Linkers** on the 31st of March!

This workshop is aimed at professionals working in data linkage and entity resolution. It’s an opportunity to: 
* Share current approaches and identify best practices
* Discuss challenges and solutions
* Build connections across organisations
* Help shape the future of data linkage in the UK

The Eventbrite registration can be found at [https://uk-dlc-data-linkers-workshop.eventbrite.co.uk](https://uk-dlc-data-linkers-workshop.eventbrite.co.uk)
