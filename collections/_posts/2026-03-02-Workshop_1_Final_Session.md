To close off the first workshop, we will report the suggested future directions and actions for the community. By far the most notable request was working to build both produced and curated resources within the community website for a variety of purposes, including 'best-practice' guidance, support for non-technical stakeholders, and many others.  
<!--more-->

### Demand for Best-Practice Guidance

One of the clearest requests was for:

- practical guidance,
- onboarding materials,
- and “linkage 101” resources.

Attendees noted that many organisations are already discussing linkage, experimenting with architectures, or considering implementation, but lack practical starting materials. Additionally, it was noted that other organisations already have partial materials such as internal training slides, methodological notes, etc. meaning the community shouldn't have to start from the ground up.

> **Proposed role of the UK Data Linkage Community:**  
> Consolidation, generalisation, and shared reuse rather than reinventing everything repeatedly.

There was particular interest in:

- consolidated best-practice guidance,
- reusable educational content,
- and lessons learned from more mature linkage teams.
